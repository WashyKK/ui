# Product image attribution

The photos on nine of the seeded products come from Wikimedia Commons, which is
used because every file on it carries an explicit free licence — the thing you
want when images go on a shop that sells things. Most are CC BY-SA, which
requires attribution and requires that derivatives stay under the same licence.

The LM2596 photo is cropped from the original, which showed three different
converter modules in one frame; a product photo of three different things is a
photo of nothing. That crop is a derivative work and stays CC BY-SA.
